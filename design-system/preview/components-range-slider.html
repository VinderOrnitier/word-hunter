4 px track painted as an inline linear-gradient (primary up to value, surface-2 after) · 18 px thumb with 2 px primary border on bg fill (so the thumb reads as a hole punched in the track). On hover the thumb fills with --wh-primary-soft and scales to 1.15; active state fills solid primary and scales to 0.96 (press feedback). Focus draws the standard --wh-shadow-glow ring around the thumb. Value-chip sits to the right of the track (mono, primary text on --wh-primary-soft).
Firefox parity · to-do at code-side rollout
Today ::-webkit-slider-thumb ships hover (scale(1.15) + soft-primary fill), focus (--wh-shadow-glow) and active (scale(0.96) + solid primary) states. ::-moz-range-thumb currently only carries the base look (size, bg, border) — no hover, no focus, no active. Mirror the three webkit pseudo-states on the moz pseudo for parity on Firefox-based browsers. Same property values, same easing.
