Replaces ProgressHeader card with a single button row. Count is mono · 4 px bar (primary fill, surface-2 track) · ach-chip shows unlocked/total with a star icon (filled when ≥ 1 unlocked) · chevron rotates 180° on expand. Streak block sits inside the expanded panel — current uses --wh-selected (a state, not a message), longest is fg-2 (subordinate). Expansion is transient (not persisted). Achievements stay always five; locked ones drop to opacity: 0.45 with an --wh-fg-4 star and a title hint of how to unlock.
