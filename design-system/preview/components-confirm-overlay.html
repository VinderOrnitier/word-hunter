Same chrome as the existing Settings footer: --wh-bg fill, 1 px top border, 0 -6px 14px rgba(0,0,0,0.25) upward shadow, 14 px padding. Slides in from +8 px with --wh-dur-base.
Two modes, one chrome.
  • save mode — buttons right-aligned, no prompt. Cancel (ghost) reverts the draft, primary commits. Used in Settings when draft !== saved.
  • confirm mode — adds a --wh-fg-2 prompt text on the left ("Clear all hunts?"), Cancel + danger primary on the right. Used in Stats for the destructive Clear action (replaces the old top-anchored ConfirmOverlay).

Why merge? The old ConfirmOverlay lived under the sticky Stats header and felt invasive — the eye lands on the table, the overlay pops above it. Moving the confirm to the bottom matches where the rest of the popup's persistent actions live (BottomActionBar, Settings footer), and lets the player keep reviewing the data while deciding. One chrome to learn, one animation to maintain.
