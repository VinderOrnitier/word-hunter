ReloadHint — appears in Play after starting a hunt
Reload the page to begin hunting.
Translucent info-blue card (--wh-info-soft fill + 1 px rgba(138, 180, 255, 0.2) border) so it reads as a soft inline notice, not a hard alert. Slides in with a 4 px translateY fade · trailing Reload CTA + × dismiss · leading info glyph carries a title nudge ("Can be disabled in Settings.").
Gated by the Reload hint switch in Settings (default on, disabled on the Settings tab). Calls chrome.scripting.executeScript on the active tab to window.location.reload() so the player doesn't need to leave the popup.

Uses --wh-info (the system's informational blue) — same token that powers the hint toast and the stats hint-dot. The hex is identical to the old --wh-hint alias; the rename clarifies that this surface speaks to the player, it doesn't carry the in-game "hint" semantic.
