IN-PAGE OVERLAYS

All Pokedex surfaces that render onto a host web page (Celebration, InPageToast, Highlighter), plus ReloadHint which lives inside the Play tab. The big design question for these was: do we lean on the device metaphor (solid raspberry chrome) or borrow slate's translucent dark glass? Pokedex commits to the device: every overlay is a solid mini-Pokedex, no blur. The brand identity carries the surface, not the host page underneath.

in-page toast · three variants

InPageToast -- small device readout pinned to the host page

A horizontal raspberry mini-device: lens (clickable, opens the popup) - message - close key. A 4px LED ribbon on the left signals the variant: cyan = hint, cream = info, yellow = auto. The shell is solid (no backdrop-blur) because the brand metaphor already gives the toast its own identity; trying to blur into the host page weakens it. Same role as slate's hw-toast; mutual-exclusion rules unchanged (info beats auto on the same page).

celebration · the one loud moment

CelebrationPopup -- REGISTERED!

The system's payoff. Fires after a successful find. Full-page scrim + centred Pokedex mini-device showing the catch on its LCD. Three variants: default solo, Auto-Continue with the Next-Up pill, and review-click with a Remove-Word ghost CTA. The Pokedex variant keeps slate's "two simultaneous found-color cues" rule -- here, the green "REGISTERED" stamp inside the LCD plus the green LED dot on the device header.

reload hint · popup-internal

ReloadHint -- appears in Play after starting a hunt

Lives inside the Play tab body, not on the host page -- shown here in a 360-wide popup slice so designers see it sitting between header and ActiveWordCard. Treated as a quiet LCD inline notice with cyan LED bezel: not a hard alert, more "the device is whispering that it would be ready, you should reload". A cream key cap on the right fires chrome.scripting.executeScript.

RELOAD TO HUNT

LCD field with cyan/navy gradient + scanlines · navy info glyph (lens-tone, not yellow) · copy "RELOAD TO HUNT" replaces slate's sentence "Reload the page to begin hunting" · cream RELOAD key · × close on the right · gated by the Reload-Hint switch in Settings (Pokedex copy: "RELOAD HINT · ON / OFF")

highlighter · brand signature

HiddenWord -- the LED rectangle behind the found word

The actual "lit-up cell" treatment on the page text. A yellow rectangle behind the word with a charcoal pixel-art outline and a soft LED glow; once found, the rectangle flips to green. Defined in themes/theme-pokedex.css as .pdx-highlight and .pdx-highlight--found. Inline flow preserved -- the highlighter wraps across line breaks the same way slate's does.
