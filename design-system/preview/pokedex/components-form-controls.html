FORM CONTROLS

Switch, NumberStepper, RangeSlider -- the atomic Settings controls in the Pokedex theme. All three are visually anchored in the LCD/key-cap metaphor: the displayed value lives in an LCD window, the inputs are cream key caps.

switch · physical slide key

Switch

Two-position slide switch: a cream key cap slides on a recessed navy track between OFF and ON. The small LED above the cap dies in OFF and lights yellow in ON, giving a second redundant cue for state on top of the physical position. Replaces slate's pill-track switch; same semantic role, same Settings fields.

number stepper · LCD digit + chevron keys

NumberStepper

Two cream chevron key caps (- on the left, + on the right) flanking a small LCD window. The window shows the value in VT323 plus the unit stamped underneath in pixel font. Replaces slate's input + stacked stepper -- the typed-number affordance disappears (pixel-grid LCDs aren't typable in real life either); on keyboard, focus on the field and use up/down arrows.

range slider · segmented LCD bar + value chip

RangeSlider

12 LCD cells in a navy housing. Filled cells show progress; the head cell glows yellow as the LED indicator. Replaces slate's gradient-painted native range -- the segmented look is honest to the LCD metaphor (real LCDs render bars as discrete cells, not smooth fills) and stays crisp at any zoom.
