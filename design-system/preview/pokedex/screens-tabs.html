SETTINGS -- fields stack + theme switcher

Vertical stack of fields, each: pixel label -- control row (helper line, where helpful) -- dashed separator. RULES -- triggered by the header info key

Header info key flips lens-tone active (cyan); the LCD body replaces the tab's normal content with the rules screen. The opening flavor line keeps slate's "a quiet game while you read" copy -- translated to VT323. A 3-row pixel-marker list summarises the game rules; the keyboard hint at the bottom uses the dedicated kbd treatment (cream key cap on the LCD).

RULES
a quiet game while you read.

Word Hunter hides one word on every page you visit. Find it to catch a creature for your collection. Hunt at your own pace -- the word waits.

  1. 01The word is hidden only in paragraphs of 30 words or more.
  2. 02One active word per session. Finish one before picking the next.
  3. 03No paragraph long enough? The page is skipped quietly.

Press CTRL F on any page to scan for the hidden word.
