Card is a primitive — a rectangle with consistent treatment. It does not carry content semantics itself; the components built on top of it (ActiveWordCard, CustomWordModal dialog, the expanded ProgressRow panel, the empty Stats state's container) compose on this base.

Recipe: background: var(--wh-surface) · border: 1px solid var(--wh-border) · border-radius: var(--wh-radius-3) · box-shadow: var(--wh-shadow-2) · padding 16 (compact) or 24 (comfortable). Cards never have a coloured left-border accent — that pattern is intentionally absent from this system.
The system uses wh-card sparingly. Most "boxes" you see in the popup are actually one of the composed components, each tuned to its specific use. If you find yourself reaching for wh-card directly, ask whether the new surface should grow into its own named component instead.
